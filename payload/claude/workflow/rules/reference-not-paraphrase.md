---
name: reference-not-paraphrase
kind: practice
applies-to: orchestrator
---

Point at a source of truth; never restate it. A brief, a ticket or a dispatch links the scope,
the mock crop, the outcome ids, the repro or the baseline by path, and names the rule by its
file. Every paraphrase is one more translation of intent, and each translation loses some: in
E17 the code was written from a ticket's restatement of a mock it never saw, every gate passed,
and the screens did not look like the mock. `/spec` and `/tickets` follow the same rule.
